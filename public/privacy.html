The short version

Kithe connects invitation-only digital Experiences to real places. The app uses approximate location while you browse and requests a precise location only when you choose to unlock a drop or, as an approved organizer, place one at your current location. Kithe does not request background location and is not designed to keep a history of where participants travel.

Kithe does not sell personal information, run behavioral advertising, or show a participant's live location to an Experience host or another participant.

3. How location works

Kithe requests foreground location in context. Approximate location supports nearby browsing. When you press Unlock, the app can send one precise fix with its accuracy and age to Kithe's server. The server evaluates proximity without writing the participant's submitted latitude and longitude to the unlock record. It records the result and related receipt information.

An approved organizer can choose coordinates for a drop or place it at the organizer's current position. Those authored coordinates are stored because they define where that drop can be found. Android platform location services process the location request. The current candidate does not have Google Maps configured; if Maps is separately approved and enabled, Google Maps will also process request, device, network, location, and map interaction information under the applicable service terms.

4. How information is used

  • Provide invitation preview, joining, maps, Trails, unlocking, Collection, rewards, and organizer tools.
  • Authenticate accounts, merge a guest account when requested, and protect private Experience data.
  • Investigate crashes, outages, fraud, abuse, reports, and reward disputes.
  • Send service notifications for Experiences you joined when notifications are enabled.
  • Create privacy-minimal pilot measurements. Raw pseudonymous pilot events are designed to expire after 180 days and are removed for the account actor during account deletion.

6. Retention and deletion

Account, membership, content, and Experience data is kept while needed to provide the account or Experience and is then deleted or deidentified under the applicable schedule. Private export download links expire after 15 minutes and export objects are deleted after 24 hours. Raw pseudonymous pilot events expire after 180 days, and their account actor link is removed during account deletion.

A pseudonymous deletion receipt is retained for 30 days. Safety case records are retained for 180 days after final case closure, then deleted or deidentified; an extension requires a logged legal hold, emergency, or active appeal. Deidentified reward, fraud, and dispute audit records are retained for 180 days after the Experience ends, with account linkage removed on deletion; an extension requires a documented legal, tax, or dispute hold.

Kithe currently has no scheduled Firestore backup and no Firestore point-in-time recovery. After Kithe requests deletion, residual data in Google's provider systems may take up to 180 days to be deleted unless applicable law requires storage. This is a provider deletion-completion period, not a Kithe restoration window. Crashlytics retains crash reports and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems. Kithe's default Google Cloud application and service logs are retained for 30 days; required administrative and system audit logs are retained for 400 days. As of the 2026-08-26 configuration review, no optional Crashlytics BigQuery/Cloud Logging integration or custom external log sink was linked. [RECHECK THESE SETTINGS IMMEDIATELY BEFORE PUBLICATION.]

7. Your choices

  • You can decline location permission. Nearby distance and unlocking will be limited.
  • You can disable Kithe notifications in the app or Android settings.
  • You can create a private data export from Account → Privacy and data → Your data.
  • You can permanently delete your account in the same area or follow the instructions on the account-deletion page.
  • You can report content or block a host using the controls provided in Kithe.

8. Children and pilot availability

Kithe Pilot 1 is available only in the United States and is intended only for people aged 18 and over. It is not designed for children. Do not create an account or use Pilot 1 if you are under 18.

9. Security and changes

Kithe uses access controls, encrypted network transport, app-integrity checks, and server-authoritative unlock decisions. No system can guarantee absolute security. Material changes to this notice should receive a new version and effective date; where required, Kithe will ask users to review and accept the updated policies.

10. Contact

Email support@kitheapp.com with privacy, access, correction, export, or deletion questions. Do not send passwords, verification codes, precise location, or unnecessary identity documents by email. Kithe will acknowledge emailed privacy or deletion requests within five business days and complete a verified deletion request within 30 calendar days unless a disclosed legal exception applies.
