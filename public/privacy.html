The short version

Kithe connects invitation-only digital Experiences to real places. The app uses approximate location while you browse and requests a precise location only when you choose to unlock a drop or, as an approved organizer, place one at your current location. Kithe does not request background location and is not designed to keep a history of where participants travel.

Kithe does not sell personal information, run behavioral advertising, or show a participant's live location to an Experience host or another participant.

3. How location works

Kithe requests foreground location in context. Approximate location supports nearby browsing. When you press Unlock, the app can send one precise fix with its accuracy and age to Kithe's server. The server evaluates proximity without writing the participant's submitted latitude and longitude to the unlock record. It records the result and related receipt information.

An approved organizer can choose coordinates for a drop or place it at the organizer's current position. Those authored coordinates are stored because they define where that drop can be found. Google Maps and platform location services also process request, device, network, and interaction information under their applicable terms.

4. How information is used

  • Provide invitation preview, joining, maps, Trails, unlocking, Collection, rewards, and organizer tools.
  • Authenticate accounts, merge a guest account when requested, and protect private Experience data.
  • Investigate crashes, outages, fraud, abuse, reports, and reward disputes.
  • Send service notifications for Experiences you joined when notifications are enabled.
  • Create privacy-minimal pilot measurements. Raw pseudonymous pilot events are designed to expire after 180 days and are removed for the account actor during account deletion.

5. Service providers and other recipients

Kithe uses service providers to operate the app and site, including Google services for Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, App Check/Play Integrity, Remote Config, Maps, and Android platform services; and Cloudflare for the website, DNS, and support-email routing. These providers process information under their own terms and Kithe's configured service relationship.

Experience hosts may receive Experience-level results and operational information needed to run their Experience. Kithe's approved design does not provide them with a participant's exact live position or travel path. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to address an emergency, or to investigate and prevent abuse, subject to an approved process.

6. Retention and deletion

Account and Experience data is kept while needed to provide Kithe and for the approved retention periods. Current engineering defaults include: private export objects deleted after 24 hours; export download links expiring after 15 minutes; pseudonymous deletion receipts retained for 30 days; and raw pilot events expiring after 180 days. These periods require final privacy/legal approval.

When an account is deleted, Kithe deletes or deidentifies account-linked data according to the account-deletion process. Safety reports can remain after reporter identity is replaced with a deletion pseudonym. Deidentified aggregate results, required fraud or reward records, legal records, and provider backups may remain for approved limited periods. 7. Your choices

  • You can decline location permission. Nearby distance and unlocking will be limited.
  • You can disable Kithe notifications in the app or Android settings.
  • You can create a private data export from Account → Privacy and data → Your data.
  • You can permanently delete your account in the same area or follow the instructions on the account-deletion page.
  • You can report content or block a host using the controls provided in Kithe.
