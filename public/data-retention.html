Deletion requests

In-app deletion requires recent reauthentication and explicit confirmation. A person who cannot use the app may email support@kitheapp.com from the address associated with the account. Kithe will acknowledge an emailed privacy or deletion request within five business days and complete a verified deletion within 30 calendar days unless a disclosed legal exception applies.

Holds and exceptions

Any extension must use the specific exception listed in the schedule and be documented, limited, and reviewed. Kithe does not use an undefined “keep as long as necessary” period for the safety, reward, fraud, dispute, or deletion-receipt categories.

Before publication, verify the individual operator wording and authorized approver, recheck production provider/export settings, approve the exact final wording, assign the final policy version/effective date, and complete the separately authorized HTTPS and signed-build checks.
