Delete your Kithe account

Engineering draft · Updated 2026-08-14

Draft — not approved for publication The deletion behavior is implemented locally, but the support response target and retained-data exceptions below require owner/privacy approval and a production rehearsal.

Deleting your account is permanent. Uninstalling Kithe does not delete your account.

Delete in the Android app

  1. Open Kithe and choose Account.
  2. Under Privacy and data, choose Your data.
  3. Reauthenticate with your password or Google account.
  4. Type DELETE.
  5. Choose Permanently delete account.

The app returns a deletion receipt when the request completes.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access the app, email Kithe from the address associated with your account. Use the subject “Kithe account deletion request.” Support will provide a proportionate identity-verification step before deletion.

Email a deletion request

Do not email your password, one-time verification code, precise location, government ID, or any sensitive document unless Kithe first provides an approved secure verification method.

What the deletion process removes

  • Your authentication account, profile, username mapping, notification tokens, memberships, private Collection and progress data.
  • Drops and media you own, subject to the approved organizer/Experience closure policy.
  • Your identifier from other reaction, report, collection, reward, and pilot-event records where the implemented deletion contract applies.
  • Account-linked raw pilot events and identity aliases.

What may be retained

  • A pseudonymous deletion receipt for the proposed 30-day troubleshooting window.
  • A safety report after the reporter account identifier is replaced, for the approved safety/legal window.
  • Deidentified aggregate Experience results and records required for an approved fraud, reward, dispute, tax, security, or legal purpose.
  • Copies in provider backups until the approved maximum backup-restoration window ends.

Before publication, approve and insert the safety, reward/dispute, legal, and backup windows. Do not publish “as long as necessary” without a documented schedule.

Timing and confirmation

In-app deletion is designed to complete during the confirmed request. Email requests will receive an acknowledgment within [APPROVED SUPPORT TARGET] and will be completed within [APPROVED DELETION TARGET] after identity verification, unless an approved legal exception applies. Kithe will send confirmation when processing is complete.

Need help first?

If the problem is sign-in or account recovery and you do not want deletion, say so in your message. Kithe will not treat a general support request as permission to delete an account.
