## Structure comparison

| Structure | Hawaiʻi description | Two-owner consequence | Kithe fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| General partnership | Two or more people carry on a business as co-owners for profit; each partner is personally liable for all partnership debts. | Simple concept, but app, privacy, contract, incident, and reward liabilities can reach the partners personally. | Not recommended as the default without specific legal advice. |
| Limited liability partnership (LLP) | A general partnership that files a qualification for limited liability. Hawaiʻi requires the partnership registration and LLP qualification filings. | Preserves partnership form with a liability election, but still requires a partnership agreement and professional review of the protection's scope. | Viable if counsel prefers partnership law for a specific reason. |
| Two-member LLC | A legal entity distinct from its members. Hawaiʻi allows member-managed or manager-managed operation and requires a Hawaiʻi registered agent. | Clear entity ownership and account/IP assignment; two-member domestic LLC defaults to partnership federal tax treatment unless it elects otherwise. | Recommended default to evaluate for Kithe; final choice belongs to both owners and their advisers. |

Current official filing facts:

- Hawaiʻi LLC Articles of Organization use Form LLC-1, require an LLC suffix in the exact
  name, a principal/mailing address, a Hawaiʻi registered agent and street address,
  organizer information, management choice, and member/manager information as applicable.
- The current LLC-1 filing fee is $50 and nonrefundable. The LLC annual report is currently
  $15 on paper or $12.50 online. Filed information is open to public inspection.
- An LLP currently requires GP-1 and LLP-1; the base filing fees are $15 and $25,
  respectively, plus the State Archives fee. Hawaiʻi says the filings are due within
  30 days after formation.
- The IRS says a domestic LLC with at least two members is treated as a partnership for
  federal income tax unless it elects corporate treatment. Form the state entity before
  applying for an EIN; the IRS EIN application is free.

Verify all fees and requirements again at filing time.

## Decisions both owners should sign before filing

1. **Legal form and exact name** — for example, `Kithe LLC` only if the name and form are
   approved and available. Domain ownership does not establish entity or trademark rights.
2. **Ownership percentages** — do not assume 50/50; record contributions and vesting, if any.
3. **Management** — member-managed or manager-managed; designate authority for contracts,
   policy approval, banking, Play, Firebase, Cloudflare, and incident response.
4. **Reserved decisions** — require both owners for equity changes, debt, sale/transfer,
   major spending, privacy-policy changes, production data migration, and dissolution.
5. **Deadlock and departure** — voting tie, voluntary exit, disability/death, removal for
   cause, valuation, buyout, and IP/account handoff.
6. **Intellectual property** — assign the Kithe code, design, domain, brand assets, Firebase
   configuration, and related work to the formed entity with a written schedule of assets.
7. **Confidentiality and data duties** — access, breach response, user-data handling,
   deletion, backups, and return of credentials/equipment on departure.
8. **Profit/loss and tax treatment** — obtain CPA advice; designate the tax matters contact
   and recordkeeping owner.
9. **Registered agent and public addresses** — choose deliberately because filed records
   are public. Do not casually publish either owner's home address.
10. **Operating agreement** — both owners sign an agreement tailored to the chosen form
    before material contracts, public launch, or revenue.

## Safe sequence

1. Both owners choose the form and sign off on the governance outline.
2. A Hawaiʻi attorney/CPA reviews liability, tax, ownership, IP, and the operating agreement.
3. Recheck the Hawaiʻi business registry and perform appropriate trademark clearance for
   the exact legal name.
4. Stop for explicit authorization before filing or paying Hawaiʻi DCCA.
5. After the state filing is accepted, apply directly through the IRS for the free EIN.
6. Open entity banking/accounting and document each owner's contribution.
7. Request a D-U-N-S Number directly from Dun & Bradstreet; D&B currently states it is free.
8. Verify `kitheapp.com` for the Play organization flow, create/verify the organization
   payments profile, then use Google's official personal-to-organization conversion process.
9. Transfer or role-separate operational assets: domain/Cloudflare, Firebase/Google Cloud,
   Play, source control, support inbox, signing keys, and billing. Do not share one person's
   password; invite named accounts with least privilege.
10. Insert the accepted legal name and formation data into Kithe's policy bundle, close the
    remaining retention/provider checks, then obtain separate publication/submission approval.
