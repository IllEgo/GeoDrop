Recovery verification:
1. Obtain the sealed envelope from Micah and the RECOVERY_BACKUP drive through the authorized

3. Use its store password to extract the key archive.
4. Confirm the two payload SHA-256 values above.
5. Open the JKS with keytool using alias kithe-play-upload and both recovered passwords.
6. Export/inspect the public certificate and confirm the colon-separated fingerprint above.
7. Record only the date, verifier, storage-system identifiers, and pass/fail result. Never
