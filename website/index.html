Location & privacy

Location is used with a purpose.

Kithe uses approximate location to show what is nearby and requests precise location only when you choose to unlock a drop.

The app does not track a continuous location history. Clear prompts explain when location access is needed and what it enables.

For hosts

Connect a digital story to a real place.

Build an Experience, place drops along a Trail, and invite guests with a code. Kithe is being prepared for small pilots with event teams, local businesses, and place-based programs.
